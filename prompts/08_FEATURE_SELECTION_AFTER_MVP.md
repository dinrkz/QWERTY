# Prompt — Feature selection after MVP

```text
Read CASE.md, FEATURE_BACKLOG.md, acceptance evidence, and the current implementation. Confirm the MVP works before selecting additions. Establish actual remaining time and reserve enough for final tests, documentation, slides, rehearsal, recording, and upload.

Recommend at most two additions whose likely user/demo benefit justifies data, implementation, and regression risk. Prefer stronger relevance, context, explanations, or usability. Creativity alone does not outweigh a broken core flow.

For each IMPLEMENT candidate give smallest scope, affected rubric criterion, evidence/data availability, estimate with assumptions, dependencies, risk, and exact acceptance check. Put everything else in DEFER/REJECT. Do not invent scores or exact weights. If time or the core is not verified, prioritize that work instead of optional implementation.
```

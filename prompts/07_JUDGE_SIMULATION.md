# Prompt — Judge simulation

```text
Act as a Build with AI judge starting only from README.md and the submitted artifacts. Use a clean environment where practical; disclose any limits to that simulation.

Attempt setup, data preparation, startup, and the documented short walk, first-time visitor discovery, and coffee/rest-stop scenarios. Check whether a visitor unfamiliar with Astana understands the explanations and whether walking/time assumptions are honest. Retain child-outing, airport, and nighttime examples as secondary robustness checks. Change a location or hard constraint to see whether results adapt. Inspect source evidence and unknown hours/amenities. Try a no-match input and provider failure.

Evaluate in the supplied order: relevance/usefulness, context, explanation quality, UX, creativity, ethics/privacy, completeness. Do not invent numerical weights. Confirm a real RAG demonstration and automated collection evidence.

Inspect the slides, scenario/limitations document, and accessible YouTube link. Report concrete evaluation blockers, unsupported claims, misleading status, and missing deliverables with severity, reproduction steps, evidence, and the smallest fix.
```
